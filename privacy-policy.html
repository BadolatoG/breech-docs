Privacy Policy — Breech

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your display name, profile photo, firearm collection data you enter (make, model, caliber, photos, notes, values), posts and content you share in the Community feed, and your Vault Showcase settings if enabled.

2. Authentication

Breech uses Sign in with Apple for account creation and login. We do not receive or store your Apple ID password.

3. Location Data

If you use the Ranges & Shops feature, Breech accesses your device's location to find nearby gun ranges and shops. Location data is used only to power this search and is not stored on our servers beyond the duration of your search session.

4. What We Never Collect or Show Publicly

Serial numbers and financial information (purchase price, total invested) associated with your firearms are never shown to other users, regardless of your Showcase settings, and firearm serial numbers are never transmitted to our servers at all — they remain local to your device only.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate core app features: displaying your Vault, powering the Community feed, enabling Vault Showcase, and providing customer support.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure cloud database provider, with row-level security ensuring only you can access or modify your own data.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers.

8. Your Choices

You can edit or delete your firearm entries, posts, and Showcase settings at any time within the app. You can disable Vault Showcase at any time to make your collection private again.

9. Children's Privacy

Breech is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to breechtheapp@gmail.com.
